## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 💧 [Borehole & Water Quality Analysis](https://github.com/jifara2024/borehole-water-quality-analysis)
End-to-end data analysis project on 60 water facilities across 7 Ethiopian regions. Includes exploratory data analysis, correlation analysis, and a linear regression model (**R² = 0.98**) predicting water conductivity from TDS — enabling faster, cheaper field-based water quality screening. Delivered as both a full analysis notebook and a stakeholder-ready insights presentation.
**Tools:** Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn, scikit-learn

### 🏢 [Urban Water Utility MIS Portfolio](https://github.com/jifara2024/Urban-Water-Utility-MIS-Portfolio)
Integrated Water Utility MIS implementation supporting digital transformation for urban water utilities in Ethiopia — covering reporting systems, GIS integration, analytics dashboards, training support, and stakeholder coordination across multiple utility sites.
**Tools:** Python, GIS, MIS Systems, Reporting & Analytics

### 🌊 [Groundwater Quality Modeling – Borena Zone](https://github.com/jifara2024/Groundwater-Quality-Modeling-Borena)
Groundwater quality assessment across Borena Zone using geostatistical interpolation (kriging) in ArcGIS, identifying spatial contamination patterns to support borehole siting and water safety planning.
**Tools:** ArcGIS, Geostatistics, SPSS, Spatial Interpolation

### 🌧️ [River Flood Maximum Flow Modeling](https://github.com/jifara2024/River-Flood-Maximum-Flow-Modeling)
Hydrological flood frequency analysis combining rainfall-runoff modeling, return period estimation, and GIS-based watershed characterization to support flood risk assessment and infrastructure planning.
**Tools:** Hydrological Modeling, GIS, Statistical Analysis

### 📄 [HTML Regulation Parser](https://github.com/jifara2024/HTML-Regulation-Parser)
Python-based parser that extracts structured information from regulatory HTML documents, automating a task that would otherwise require manual review.
**Tools:** Python, Web Scraping, Data Parsing
